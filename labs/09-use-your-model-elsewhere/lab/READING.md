## Test your model in a new situation

Choose the transfer task in the learner kit: turn community event notes into an accessibility-aware visit plan. It differs from a buying decision. You must adapt the output criteria instead of copying the desk columns.

Before starting, predict the needed inputs, actions, output, and one uncertainty. Define the constraints: maximum time, access requirements, and what counts as an unsupported claim. Then run the task or analyze a supplied example if execution is unavailable.

Compare prediction with result. Identify a gap in your model and redraw the relevant part. The aim is calibrated understanding: knowing what you can explain, what you have tested, and what remains uncertain. Broad claims about predicting the future are unnecessary.
