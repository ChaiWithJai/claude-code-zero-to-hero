# Use your model somewhere new

Adapt your method to a new task and revise the model from evidence.

## Try it

1. Read inputs/event-notes.txt. Do not reuse the desk columns without thinking.
2. Define the output criteria for a 90-minute hands-on visit with step-free access confirmed.
3. Predict the inputs and actions, then attempt the task without a step-by-step prompt.
4. Check two claims. Preserve missing dates, prices, availability, and access information.
5. In outputs/NOTES.md explain what you adapted, what surprised you, and how your model changed.

## Keep

Save your output in `outputs/`. Keep your prediction, source checks, model revision, and evidence route in `outputs/NOTES.md`. Your notes can stay private.

## Check

- Did your criteria change for this task?
- Were two claims checked against event source IDs?
- Did observation lead to a specific model revision?

The launcher offers hints, examples, and a work check. A check of an open-ended note is a prompt for review, not a grade.
