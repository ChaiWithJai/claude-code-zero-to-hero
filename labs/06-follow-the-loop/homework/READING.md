## Follow the loop, not the spectacle

A workflow can prescribe a route through known steps. An agent can choose its next action using the goal and feedback. Real systems combine these patterns. The useful distinction is who controls the steps, not whether the system is always on or has a human-like identity.

Use the local research example as a slow demonstration. First predict which file the agent needs. Observe the read. Predict whether it now has enough information. Then inspect what it creates. The underlying application may take a different valid route from your prediction; explain the difference rather than treating every mismatch as failure.

Your instructor story is a role model for learning through small models and tests. It is not a promise that participants will acquire the instructor’s entire engineering background in one session. What transfers is the habit: locate, predict, act, inspect, update.
