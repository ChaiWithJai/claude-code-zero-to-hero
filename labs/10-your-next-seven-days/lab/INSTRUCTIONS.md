# Choose your next seven days

Leave with an honest capability record and three opportunities for continued use.

## Try it

1. Compare your starting model with the one you can now explain.
2. Give a short walkthrough: your center, a sidekick, access, a checked result, a revision, and a next step.
3. Name three specific use opportunities and their triggers in the next seven days.
4. Add a success measure, stop rule, approved fallback, and review point.
5. Distinguish confidence, what you executed, and use you will only be able to observe later.

## Keep

Save your output in `outputs/`. Keep your prediction, source checks, model revision, and evidence route in `outputs/NOTES.md`. Your notes can stay private.

## Check

- Are the three opportunities concrete enough to start?
- Does the plan include a stop rule and a review point?
- Did you keep analyzed and executed evidence separate?

The launcher offers hints, examples, and a work check. A check of an open-ended note is a prompt for review, not a grade.
