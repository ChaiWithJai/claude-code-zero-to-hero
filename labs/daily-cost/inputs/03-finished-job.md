# NOTE-03: How I decide the job finished

Jai's teaching method: predict before acting, inspect the result against the source, then retain a specific improvement. Keep the meaningful judgment with the human and delegate bounded preparation. A usable handout needs source-linked claims, a runnable practice exercise, and feedback. Record active setup, review and repair time separately from waiting. Count failed attempts in the resources spent, and accepted outputs in the denominator. An unknown charge remains unknown.

Source: Jai's course-design decisions and this class brief, 2026-10-20. The $60/hour time value and eight-minute example are illustrative assumptions, not observed learner data.
