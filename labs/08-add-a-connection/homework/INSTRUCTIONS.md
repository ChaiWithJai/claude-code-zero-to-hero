# Homework: Choose a useful connection

Complete an access inventory for one real work task. Resolve the permission question through your normal process before testing.

You can open this activity directly. Use the supplied inputs, an appropriately usable example of your own, or a written simulation if execution access is unavailable. Record which route you used.

## Keep

Save your output in `outputs/`. Keep your prediction, source checks, model revision, and evidence route in `outputs/NOTES.md`. Your notes can stay private.

## Check

- Does the connection unlock something your wish needs?
- Is the status honestly planned, blocked, or tested?
- Can useful work continue with a local file?

The launcher offers hints, examples, and a work check. A check of an open-ended note is a prompt for review, not a grade.
