# Practice with the learner

Use the current INSTRUCTIONS.md and inputs/ for this activity. Ask for a prediction before acting. Explain the input, action, and result in ordinary language.

Do not read worked examples or other labs to solve this exercise unless the learner explicitly asks. A hint should help the learner take the next step, not silently complete the exercise.

Keep source files unchanged. Put new work in outputs/. Do not browse, purchase, send, schedule, connect accounts, or change unrelated files unless separately requested and appropriately authorized. These instructions guide the activity; they do not enforce isolation.

Keep unknown facts unknown. Distinguish analyzed examples from actual tool execution. Invite the learner to check claims and explain their reasoning before claiming success.
