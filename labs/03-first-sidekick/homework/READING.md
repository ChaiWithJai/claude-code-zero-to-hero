## Direct your first sidekick

Use the fictional desk case first. Read buyer-brief.txt and the three source cards. The brief contains priorities and one hard constraint: the desk must fit within 105 cm width. A cheaper or more feature-rich option is not useful if it fails that boundary.

A delegation brief contains the goal, context, inputs, output, boundaries, and checks. Ask for a CSV comparison and a short recommendation note. Require source IDs in each row and “unknown” for facts not supplied. A model can generate plausible details; that is why the checks are part of the request.

In the agentic loop, the application can select tools, inspect their results, and choose another step. The task is still bounded by your goal and what the environment allows. Watch one action at a time and predict where its result will appear. You remain responsible for deciding whether the evidence is sufficient.
