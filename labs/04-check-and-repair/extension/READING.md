## Inspect the result before trusting it

Open outputs separately from the agent conversation. Check a price, a dimension, and whether the hard constraint was applied. Source IDs make the comparison traceable. They do not prove the facts are correct until you inspect the referenced source.

Use four quality dimensions: accuracy, fit to your method, usefulness for the question, and correction effort. Rate each from one to five using the anchors in the binder. A four means the named criterion is met with only a minor correction; a five means it is met and independently checked. Do not average away an incorrect hard constraint.

Feedback is a concrete mismatch and an invitation to repair it. “Bad answer” does not tell your sidekick what to change. “This option is wider than my stated limit; mark it ineligible and revise the recommendation” identifies the evidence, the rule, and the repair. Inspect the repair before moving on.
