# Choose your wish

Choose a meaningful wish that fits a short experiment.

## Try it

1. Write three things you wish you had help accomplishing.
2. Choose one visible result you could inspect in a short sitting.
3. Name the decision you want to keep for yourself.
4. Recall two low-stakes decisions: separate how you felt before choosing, others’ influence, and the eventual outcome. Keep the reflection private if you prefer.
5. Write one tentative pattern, one uncertainty, and the next experiment you want to try.

## Keep

Save your output in `outputs/`. Keep your prediction, source checks, model revision, and evidence route in `outputs/NOTES.md`. Your notes can stay private.

## Check

- Is the result small enough to inspect?
- Can you explain why it matters and what remains your judgment?

The launcher offers hints, examples, and a work check. A check of an open-ended note is a prompt for review, not a grade.
